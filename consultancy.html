The Work Itself

No Surprises

How We Work

Fixed scope, written deliverables, and an exit at every stage. If the honest answer is that you don't need us, you'll hear it in step one.

01

Scoping Call — Free

Thirty minutes on what you're trying to do. You leave with a straight answer on feasibility whether or not we work together.

02

Written Proposal

Scope, deliverables, timeline, and cost in writing before anything starts. Fixed price where the work is well defined.

03

Build in the Open

You get repo access from day one and a written update every week. No reveal at the end — you watch it get built.

04

Handover

Documentation, a runbook, and a walkthrough with whoever maintains it next. The goal is that you don't need us again.
